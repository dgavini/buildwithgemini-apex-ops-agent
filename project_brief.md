# My agent: apex-ops-agent
One-liner: A conversational operations agent that helps company employees and managers complete onboarding, process expense claims, and triage IT incidents with a catalog of operational tasks and resources.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers user employee profile, department preferences, past submitted expense claims, and ongoing incident tickets.
- Tools: Look up and submit onboarding requests, calculate and file expense claims with policy evaluation, log and mitigate IT incident reports, read and write operations tasks in Firestore.
- Catalog/UI: Collection of company operational tasks, expense policies, employee directories, and system incident feeds rendered as cards and tables.
- Image gen: Generates employee badge portraits and incident system architecture topology diagrams.
- Sandbox: Computes expense totals, tax breakdowns, and incident SLA countdown metrics.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for financial and SLA calculations, Cloud Trace
